As of 9 October 2026 on Oddzy, the Champions League 2026-27 winner odds put Barcelona in front at 22.5¢ — a bit better than one in five — with Bayern Munich at 14.5¢, Arsenal at 13.5¢ and holders Paris Saint-Germain at 12.5¢. One matchday of eight in the league phase has been played, and the final is on 5 June 2027 in Madrid.

Prices below are from Oddzy at 07:13 UTC on 9 October 2026. Each is the price of a share that pays $1 if that club wins the competition and $0 if it does not.

The bookmaker column is the consensus Oddschecker reported on 25 August 2026, before the league phase began. A bookmaker’s implied percentages include its margin, so they run slightly high across the board.

## Why does the market rate Barcelona above PSG?

The honest answer is that the price does not tell you. A prediction market price is the level at which two traders last agreed, and on 9 October 2026 Barcelona’s market had traded $904 in 24 hours. That is a handful of orders, not a crowd.

PSG is the only favourite with real activity: $21,256 in the same window. So the cleanest reading of the table is narrower than “the market prefers Barcelona.” It is that traders willing to put money on the holders right now value them at about one in eight, below the one-in-six bookmakers offered in August.

Arsenal’s 13.5¢ is the weakest number on the board. It did not trade at all in the 24 hours to this snapshot, so it is a quote nobody has tested. On a book this thin, the price you can actually buy at sits above the card, which is [why an order fills at a worse price than the one you saw](/learn/why-did-my-order-fill-at-a-worse-price).

## Where is the money on this board actually going?

Into the clubs nobody expects to win. Of $1.24 million traded across all 36 winner markets in the 24 hours to 9 October 2026, about $1 million went through clubs priced under 1¢. Shakhtar Donetsk, at 0.15¢, traded $425,514 on its own — about 16 times the top seven combined — and has traded $28.1 million since it opened.

That volume is not a forecast. It is mostly people buying NO at 99.85¢ and waiting, which is a yield trade with a specific risk profile, worked through in [whether 99¢ shares are free money](/learn/buying-99-cent-shares). The practical consequence: the board’s headline volume says nothing about how reliable the favourites’ prices are.

## Does the board add up to 100¢?

Not quite. The 36 prices sum to 103.05¢ on 9 October 2026. Part of that is a floor effect: a card cannot show much less than a fraction of a cent, so the 14 clubs priced at 0.25¢ or less carry 2.6¢ between them, which is almost certainly more than their combined chance.

Do not read the extra three cents as a free trade. Selling every club at the card would require bids at the card, and on the thin legs there are none.

## When does a winner market actually settle?

Each club’s market resolves Yes if it wins the competition and No as soon as winning becomes impossible under UEFA’s rules. That makes elimination the event that matters. The league phase ends on 27 January 2027; the top eight go straight to the round of 16, the next 16 play off, and the bottom 12 are out. Those 12 markets can resolve No in late January rather than in June.

Check the close time on the specific market before you buy. On Oddzy, half of these markets list a close of 30 May 2027 and half 20 June 2027, while the final is on 5 June. The rules on the market page are the authority, which is the case for [reading the resolution rules before the price](/learn/read-the-resolution-rules-first).
