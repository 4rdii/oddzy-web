A favourite near one in five is not timid. The NBA has had eight different champions in the eight seasons since 2019 — Toronto, the Lakers, Milwaukee, Golden State, Denver, Boston, Oklahoma City and New York — and the price reflects that.

## Why are the Spurs favourites after losing the Finals?

The Spurs reached the 2026 Finals and lost 4-1 to the Knicks, and the market reads that run as the stronger signal than the result. They are also its pick for the regular season: on 9 November 2026 the Western Conference #1 seed market priced San Antonio at 44.5¢, almost twice the Thunder’s 25¢. Basketball-Reference had both teams at 8-2 on 7 November.

## Why have the Thunder slipped to 15.5¢?

Mostly because of one injury. Jalen Williams fractured his left fibula and dislocated his ankle in a win over the Clippers on 25 October 2026, and the Thunder said after surgery that he will be re-evaluated before the end of the regular season. Guard Cason Wallace also needs shoulder surgery. Until there is a firm return date, the Thunder price is partly a price on Williams being back for the playoffs.

## How did the 76ers get to 13.35¢?

LeBron James signed a two-year deal with Philadelphia in late July 2026, joining Joel Embiid and Tyrese Maxey, and the 76ers opened 8-2. They are also the most actively traded of the top three, at $12,459 in the 24 hours to this snapshot. In the East, the #1 seed market has Philadelphia and Boston level at 26.5¢ each, so the market sees the conference as a two-team race at the top.

## Do the conference and title markets agree?

Roughly, and the gap is informative. Dividing a team’s title price by its conference price gives the market’s implied chance of winning the Finals once it gets there:

| Team | Title | Conference | Implied Finals win |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spurs | 21.5¢ | 35¢ (West) | 61% |
| Thunder | 15.5¢ | 21.5¢ (West) | 72% |
| 76ers | 13.35¢ | 23.4¢ (East) | 57% |
| Knicks | 7.5¢ | 17.5¢ (East) | 43% |

The Thunder’s 72% is the outlier: it says Oklahoma City would be a bigger Finals favourite than San Antonio, which the title board itself contradicts. Trust the title board. The Thunder’s conference market has traded about $21,000 since it opened, against $1.38 million on its title market.

The 30 title prices sum to 104.75¢, not 100¢, mostly because cards for teams that are almost certainly out cannot show much less than a fraction of a cent. That extra 4.75¢ is not free money, because [selling every team at the card needs bids at the card](/learn/why-did-my-order-fill-at-a-worse-price). Western teams account for 61.45¢ of the total, so the market leans West for the trophy.

## When does an NBA title market settle?

Each team’s market resolves Yes if it wins the 2027 Finals and No as soon as it becomes impossible to win under NBA rules — usually when it is eliminated. A team knocked out in the first round settles in April or May. Only the finalists wait until June. If no champion is declared by 11:59 PM ET on 30 June 2027, the markets resolve to Other. The rules on each market page are the authority, which is why you [read the resolution rules before the price](/learn/read-the-resolution-rules-first).

## Before you take a position

A title position ties up money for seven months unless you sell, and you can [sell before a market resolves](/learn/can-you-sell-before-a-market-resolves) at the best bid. Most teams’ books trade a few thousand dollars a day, so check the spread before you size. Some long shots trade more than the favourites — the Dallas Mavericks, at 0.9¢, did $14,615, more than twice the Spurs — and that volume is mostly people buying NO and waiting, the trade examined in [whether 99¢ shares are free money](/learn/buying-99-cent-shares). The full board, plus seeds, awards and win totals, is on [the NBA futures topic page](/topic/nba-futures).
