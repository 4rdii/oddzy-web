NBA prediction markets turn one basketball game into a set of yes-or-no questions you can trade. Will the 76ers beat the Magic. Will the Lakers win by 7 or more. Will the two teams score 222 points between them. Each share pays $1 if the answer is yes and $0 if not, so the price in cents is the probability the market puts on it.

Most of those questions settle the way you would expect. The ones that do not are player props, and the difference is worth money.

## How do NBA prediction markets work for a single game?

Each NBA game is a cluster of independent markets: a moneyline on who wins, a ladder of point spreads, a ladder of totals, and player props on points, rebounds, assists and threes. Every market has its own order book, its own traders and its own settlement text.

As of 18 November 2026, 07:13 UTC, Oddzy listed 2,591 active NBA game markets. The 76ers against the Magic, tipping off at 7:00 PM ET that evening, was the busiest game not yet played:

| Market (76ers vs Magic) | Card price | Cheapest to buy, each side | 24h volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moneyline, 76ers / Magic | 51.5¢ / 48.5¢ | 52¢ / 49¢ | $51,534 |
| Spread, 76ers −1.5 | 49.5¢ | 50¢ / 51¢ | $4,354 |
| Total, Over 219.5 | 55.5¢ | 56¢ / 45¢ | $3,600 |
| Total, Over 221.5 | 50.5¢ | 51¢ / 50¢ | $11,236 |
| Total, Over 223.5 | 45.5¢ | 46¢ / 55¢ | $7,006 |

The prices check each other. The 76ers to win at 51.5¢ and the 76ers to win by 2 or more at 49.5¢ leave roughly 2¢ for a one-point 76ers win. Each two points up the totals ladder takes about 5¢ off the Over.

## How does an NBA spread market settle?

A spread market pays the favourite only if it wins by more than the line, and pays the other team in every other case. Spread: Lakers (−6.5) resolved to the Lakers only on a win by 7 or more points; a Lakers win by 6, or any Hornets win, resolved it to the Hornets.

Every spread and total line in the markets above is set at half a point, so there is no push — the sportsbook outcome where the margin lands exactly on the line and the stake comes back. Totals work the same way: Over 221.5 needs 222 combined points.

## Does overtime count in NBA prediction markets?

Yes. The moneyline rules say the result is the final score including overtime, and the prop rules say the entire game including all overtime periods counts. The spread and total rules refer to the game’s score and do not exclude overtime, so a game that goes to overtime is settled on what the scoreboard says when it ends.

## What happens if an NBA game is postponed?

The market waits, then splits. If a game is cancelled, does not start by 11:59 PM ET two days after its scheduled date, or is abandoned without an official result, the market resolves 50-50, every share paying 50¢. For the 76ers–Magic game, scheduled for Wednesday, the cut-off is Friday, 20 November.

One exception: if the outcome was already decided under the rules before the game was abandoned, that outcome stands. An Over already passed in the third quarter stays an Over. The rest of [how a market resolves and when you get paid](/learn/how-markets-resolve-and-when-you-get-paid) follows the same path as any other market.

## What happens to a player prop if the player sits out?

It resolves No. If the player is listed as inactive or never takes the court, the prop settles as if the player had recorded nothing. Many sportsbooks refund a prop on a player who does not play; this market does not, which is the clearest reason to [read the resolution rules before the price](/learn/read-the-resolution-rules-first).

That makes the two sides of a prop unequal:

| What happens | Over/Yes share | Under/No share |
|---|---|---|
| Player plays and clears the line | Pays $1 | Pays $0 |
| Player plays and falls short | Pays $0 | Pays $1 |
| Player is inactive or never checks in | Pays $0 | Pays $1 |

Buying the Over is a bet that the player takes the court *and* clears the line. A player can be ruled out minutes before tip-off, so check the injury report before you take an Over, not after.

Props also resolve early. The Luka Dončić Points O/U 34.5 market for 17 November named the earlier of two sources: Dončić passing 37.5 points on the NBA.com box score, or the first official game book after the game. The Moussa Diabaté 9.5 market used 12.5. Stat corrections after resolution is triggered do not count.

## Why do prop prices look so different from what I can buy?

Because most props have almost no book. As of 18 November 2026 on Oddzy, the Diabaté prop showed 8.5¢ and cost 17¢ to buy, and several Grayson Allen props showed 50¢ with no offer below $1. The card shows a midpoint; you fill at the offer, which is [why an order fills at a worse price than the one you saw](/learn/why-did-my-order-fill-at-a-worse-price).

The main lines are a different market. The Lakers −6.5 spread against the Hornets traded $1,530,129 in the 24 hours to 07:13 UTC on 18 November, the most of any market on Oddzy — nearly all of it around the game itself. A deep spread can be traded in and out of during play; a prop with no bid usually cannot, so plan to hold it to settlement or [sell it back](/learn/can-you-sell-before-a-market-resolves) only where there is a buyer.

## Before you take a position

Read the settlement text on the specific market, check who is playing, and size the stake so a total loss is survivable.
