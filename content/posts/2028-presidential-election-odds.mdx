As of 5 October 2026 on Oddzy, the 2028 presidential election odds put JD Vance in front at 20.05¢ — a one-in-five chance — with Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez at 14.8¢ and Jon Ossoff at 10.85¢. The election is 25 months away and both parties’ nominations are still open, so the board is pricing a field, not a race.

Prices below are from Oddzy at 07:13 UTC on 5 October 2026. Each one is the price of a share that pays $1 if that person wins and $0 if they don’t.

## Why is the favourite only a one-in-five chance?

Because Vance has to win twice. The market gives him 50.25¢ to win the Republican nomination, and 20.05¢ to win the presidency. Divide one by the other and you get the market’s implied chance that he wins the general election if he is the nominee: about 40%.

That conditional figure is the more useful number. It says the market is not doubting Vance as a Republican so much as doubting a Republican in 2028.

Rubio’s pair implies 59% — a stronger nominee than Vance. Both Rubio markets traded under $2,200 in the last 24 hours, so treat that as a thin-book artefact before you treat it as an opinion. A price on a market nobody is trading is the last thing someone was willing to do, not what the crowd thinks today.

## Do the party and candidate prices agree?

On the Republican side, exactly. Vance, Rubio, Tucker Carlson, Ron DeSantis, Trump and Thomas Massie sum to 34.5¢ — the Republican party price to the tenth of a cent.

On the Democratic side, no. The thirteen Democrats priced at half a cent or more sum to 50.9¢, against 64.5¢ for the party. Either the market expects the nominee to be someone who barely trades today, or the party price is stale: the Democratic party market had no trades at all in the 24 hours to this snapshot. The candidate markets are where the money is, and they are the better read.

## Why do some candidates trade far more than their price suggests?

Because their price moves on news that is not about 2028. Talarico, at 1.25¢, traded $75,778 in a day — more than Harris, and about 36 times Rubio. He is the Democratic nominee in the Texas Senate race against Ken Paxton on 3 November 2026, and a win there would make him a national name overnight. Ossoff is defending his Georgia Senate seat the same day. Those markets are, in effect, side bets on the midterms; the arithmetic of that election is in [why the House and Senate markets are so far apart](/learn/2026-midterm-markets-house-senate-gap).

## How does the 2028 market decide who won?

It resolves once the Associated Press, Fox News and NBC have all called the race for the same candidate. If the three have not agreed by 20 January 2029, it resolves to whoever is inaugurated. The election itself is on 7 November 2028.

The nomination market is narrower: it pays only if the named person wins *and accepts* the Republican nomination, and a later replacement of the nominee does not change the result. How these rules play out on a real election night is covered in [how election prediction markets decide who won](/learn/election-prediction-markets).

## What does it cost to hold a position until November 2028?

Twenty-five months of your money doing nothing else. Cash is the yardstick — tokenized Treasury funds paid roughly 4–5% a year in September 2026, as covered in [what your stablecoins actually earn](/learn/stablecoin-yield-guide).

| Position, held to the result | Price | Return if right | Per year, over 25 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| NO on Vance | 79.95¢ | 25.1% | 11.3% |
| NO on Talarico | 98.75¢ | 1.3% | 0.6% |
| YES on every priced candidate | 91.55¢ | 9.2% | 4.3% |

The middle row is the important one. Nobody locks up 98.75¢ for two years to earn 1.25¢, so nobody sells the longshots down to their fair price, and they stay expensive. A 1¢ price on a two-year market is partly the cost of time, not a 1% forecast.

The bottom row is not free money either. The 54 priced candidates sum to 91.55¢, but those are midpoints, the asks are higher, and the 8.45¢ gap over 25 months is roughly what cash pays anyway. The same logic is worked through at the near-certain end in [whether 99¢ shares are free money](/learn/buying-99-cent-shares).

## Before you take a position

You do not have to wait for the result. Every 2028 market can be [sold before it resolves](/learn/can-you-sell-before-a-market-resolves), at the best bid, which on a market trading a few hundred dollars a day can sit several cents under the card. Size for a total loss, because a named candidate who never runs simply resolves NO. The full board is on [the global elections topic page](/topic/global-elections).
